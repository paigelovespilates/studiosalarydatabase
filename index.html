before you ask

a few things worth knowing

is this cohort-based, or can I start whenever?
cohort-based. everyone moves through the same 20 lessons on the same september 15 – october 15 schedule, together, with live calls happening in real time. no rolling start.
what if I can't make it after I've paid?
life happens — if you can't do this cohort, you can transfer your spot to the next one or gift it to a fellow instructor. no refunds once the cohort starts, since lesson access begins immediately.
do I need any experience already?
this is built for newly certified instructors — you've got the training, this fills in everything the certification didn't. no prior teaching reps required.
what if I miss a live call?
calls are recorded, so you won't lose the content — but the real-time coaching (mock classes, hot-seat feedback) only happens live, so try to show up if you can.
